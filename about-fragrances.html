Understanding fragrances

A fragrance is a blend of aromatic materials designed to create a scent. People often use broad “families” to describe the overall impression of a fragrance. These descriptions are guides, rather than strict rules.

  • Fresh: often described as bright, clean or airy.
  • Woody: commonly associated with dry, earthy or timber-like impressions.
  • Floral: built around impressions associated with flowers.
  • Spicy: may feel warm, aromatic or lively.
  • Warm: often used for rounded, rich or cosy impressions.

One fragrance may sit across several families. The words used for a product do not guarantee how it will smell to you, and the current product listings do not include verified note breakdowns.

What are inspired-version fragrances?

An inspired-version fragrance is independently presented as drawing inspiration from the general scent character of another fragrance. It is a separate product, not the original designer product. It should not be confused with an official product, and Oro Liquido does not claim authorization or affiliation with referenced fragrance brands.

Names that refer to other fragrances are used to identify the inspiration shown on the supplied product material. Product composition, origin and performance have not been independently verified here.

Choosing a fragrance

Start with the types of scents you already enjoy. Think about where you plan to wear it—everyday use, work, social time or a special occasion—and whether you prefer a fresher or deeper overall impression.

Warm and humid weather can change how a fragrance feels and develops. If you are unsure, a smaller size can be a practical way to explore a scent before choosing a larger format. Individual preference is the best guide.
